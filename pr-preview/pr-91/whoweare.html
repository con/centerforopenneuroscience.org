Centroids

Yaroslav O. Halchenko

Founding Director
E-Mail +1 (603) 646-9834 Research Page CV Twitter Mastodon FaceBook GitHub LinkedIn ORCID
Research Professor of Psychological and Brain Sciences and Adjunct Professor of Computer Science at Dartmouth, official Debian Developer, contributor to many open source projects -- Yaroslav (AKA Yarik) is a lead of DANDI, DataLad, PyMVPA, NeuroDebian, and other projects. He is also currently serving on BIDS standard Steering and NWB standard technical advisory committees, and engaged in various other initiatives, projects, and efforts.

James V. Haxby

Co-Director
E-Mail Research Page CV GitHub ORCID
Dr. Haxby for many years directed the Dartmouth Brain Imaging Center (DBIC) and the Center for Cognitive Neuroscience (CCN) and cradled many of the CON projects. Dr.Haxby has an outstanding history of developing and disseminating in the open novel research methodologies, as well as influential datasets.

Cody Baker

Research Software Engineer
E-Mail Home Page GitHub LinkedIn ORCID
Data connoisseur responsible for creating many software packages through the NWB and DANDI ecosystem (NeuroConv, NWB Inspector, NWB GUIDE). Experienced in uploading several hundred terabytes of neurophysiology datasets to NIH archives. Currently working on DANDI infrastructure.

John Lee

Research Software Engineer
E-Mail GitHub LinkedIn ORCID
John brings together research imaging, open-source infrastructure, and an industry engineering perspective. His work spans NIH neuroimaging data sharing, AFNI testing infrastructure, consulting and independent software work, and reproducible biomedical image transformation. At CON, he works on building and applying the center's tools for reproducibility and provenance tracking to healthcare image data, with an interest in auditable autonomous systems for scientific research and clinical translation.
