Logos and marks of projects, organizations and services

These files are logos, wordmarks and icons of projects, organizations and
services, among them projects of the Center for Open Neuroscience itself.
Each belongs to its owner and stays under its owner's terms; it is
reproduced here only to identify what it stands for and is NOT covered by
the licenses of the rest of this repository.  No rights are granted here; to
reuse one, consult its owner's terms.  Where a logo is known to carry a
license of its own, REUSE.toml states that license instead of this one.
